— Who We Are —

Our Journey. Our Experience. Our Commitment.

Building excellence from labour contracting roots to comprehensive construction leadership since 2009.

Evolution Since 2009

Viha Group of Constructions is built on a strong foundation of experience, hard work, dedication, and an uncompromising commitment to quality. Our journey began in 2009 as a labour contracting business, growing through consistent performance and reliable execution.

As capabilities expanded, we progressed from labour contracting to material contracting, taking on greater responsibilities and delivering comprehensive turnkey construction solutions across residential, commercial, and industrial sectors.

Our Vision

To establish Viha Group as a trusted name by delivering quality-driven projects and long-term client value.

Our Mission

To execute every project with integrity, safety, and efficiency while continuously elevating our capabilities.
